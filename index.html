6 rules for safe trading

Only trade inside the Tbay appEvery trade, rate and payout should happen in the app, where your money is protected and traceable.
Never trade on WhatsApp or DMsNo real Tbay buyer or agent will ask you to trade outside the app. Anyone who does is not Tbay.Read the guide
Never share your PIN, OTP or passwordTbay staff will never ask for them, not by call, chat, email or social media.Read the guide

Know the scam

How the "outside trade" scam works

Scammers pose as Tbay buyers or agents on WhatsApp
  1. 1
    They contact you firstOften quoting real trade details to look official.
  2. 2
    They offer a "better rate" outside the appThe first few trades may even go smoothly, to win your trust.
  3. 3
    Then they disappear with your cardsNo app, no record, no way to get your money back.
The rule is simple: if it's outside the Tbay app, it's not Tbay.
